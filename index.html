OpenLKA: Real-World Lane Keeping Assist

A multimodal dataset for LKA with synchronized CAN, front-view video, and OpenPilot outputs — enabling benchmarking, safety analysis, and infrastructure readiness studies.

Explore Dataset LKA Failure Analysis Early-Warning System Publications

Multimodal Controller-aware Frame-aligned Real-world diversity

OpenLKA is an open, large-scale dataset focused on real-world Lane Keeping Assist (LKA). It aggregates 389.1 hours across 62 production vehicle models and 52 drivers, synchronizing CAN signals with front-view video and OpenPilot (OP) perception/trajectory outputs. The dataset powers two companion efforts: LKAnalysis for failure-causation analytics and LKAlert for multimodal early-warning.
